# MR.B2BEAST.

Build leverage, not to-do lists.

Hi, I'm Tristan. 20 years in retail, 10+ years in SaaS growth, 5 years on Bali. First SaaS in 2016: an Instagram bot. It got to 5k MRR, then Instagram changed the rules.

Today I build [Gaapp](https://gaapp.io) in public, about one hour a day, next to a full-time job. This profile shows what shipped and what the numbers say.

## What I'm building

**[Gaapp.io](https://gaapp.io)**
One yearly goal, broken down to quarter, month, week and today's step. Live since 2026-09-11, in English and German. Has a REST API and an MCP server, so your AI assistant can read and check off your goals. Roadmap and changelog: [gaapp-io/roadmap](https://github.com/gaapp-io/roadmap).

**[mrb2beast.com](https://mrb2beast.com)**
My English site. Articles that check popular productivity studies against their data, a weekly newsletter, and [open stats](https://mrb2beast.com/stats). Moved off Lovable to a Hetzner server in October 2026, built on with Claude Code since.

## What runs on its own

Most of the plumbing runs as Claude Code jobs on a small Hetzner server. New articles go out as posts, the newsletter is drafted on Monday, every sent issue lands in the archive, the monthly numbers land in a sheet. I review over Telegram and stop what I don't like.

Replies under other people's posts are drafts at most, I post them myself. No automated follows, reposts or mails to individual people. Nothing on LinkedIn.
